## Rules followed for every entry above

- No email address is presented as confirmed unless it appeared as directly quotable
  snippet text — two candidates had a plausible address surface only through the search
  tool's own synthesis, and both are flagged as **unverified, confirm independently**
  rather than repeated as fact.

- No personalization invents a contact's name — where a real individual's name was implied
  only by synthesis rather than a confirmed snippet (e.g. Provident Prepper's authors), the
  draft addresses the site's team generically instead of a possibly-wrong personal name.
- Every pitch offers a specific, real PowerMatchLab asset (a named calculator, the dataset,
  or the comparator) — never a vague "check out our site."
