# Pinterest — 12 Ready-to-Post Pins

General notes for whoever posts these:
- **Visual proposal** describes what the pin graphic should show — none of these exist as image files yet; design them in Canva/Figma using the site's existing navy/cyan brand colors (see `src/lib/og-template.tsx` for the exact palette) and pull real numbers only from the linked page.
- Every pin's destination URL is a real, live page on the site — never a bare homepage link when a more specific page fits the pin's topic.
- No prices, no "best," no unverified superlatives anywhere in pin copy, per this project's standing rule.
- Reuse ideas across platforms, but every pin below has its own wording — none of these are copy-pasted from the Facebook/LinkedIn or video scripts.

---

- **Title:** Watts vs. Watt-Hours: The One Mix-Up That Gets You the Wrong Power Station
- **Description:** Watts is a rate (like speed). Watt-hours is a total (like distance). Get this backwards and you'll buy a power station that either can't start your device or runs out too fast. Free breakdown + calculator.
- **Visible pin text:** "Watts ≠ Watt-Hours" (large) / "Here's the difference that actually matters" (small)
- **Destination URL:** /guides/watts-vs-watt-hours
- **Visual proposal:** Split graphic — left side "100W" with a speedometer icon, right side "100Wh" with a battery/fuel-gauge icon, navy background, cyan accent line down the middle
- **Alt text:** Diagram explaining the difference between watts (a rate of power) and watt-hours (a total amount of energy) for portable power stations.
