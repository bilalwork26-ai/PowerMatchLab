   forum likely restricts outside links for new accounts, the draft below is written so it
   still stands alone as a genuinely useful answer with the link removed or added only as
   a "happy to share if useful" follow-up, never as the point of the post.
2. **Answer the actual question first**, in the poster's own terms — every draft leads with
   real, useful information, not a pitch.
3. **Disclosure, every time.** Every draft identifies the poster as being with
   PowerMatchLab when mentioning the tool — never as an "independent user" who happens to
   have found it.
4. **No invented experience.** None of these drafts claim personal ownership of a specific
   CPAP model, RV, or Starlink kit, or a specific test result "we" ran — they stick to the
   same published formulas/facts used across this campaign, phrased as general knowledge,
   not as "I tried this and it worked for me."
5. **One post per real thread found**, never a templated reply mass-posted across many
   threads with only the link changed.

- **Draft answer:**
  > If you're pairing solar with CPAP battery backup, one distinction worth being precise about: your solar input reduces the *daily energy* your battery has to supply over 24 hours — it doesn't reduce the *instant* power draw when your machine (or its humidifier heater) kicks on. Sizing the battery just off "average daily solar minus average daily use" without checking the instant draw separately can leave you short at the exact moment it matters.
