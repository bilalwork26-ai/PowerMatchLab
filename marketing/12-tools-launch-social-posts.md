### FB Post 5 — CPAP, AC vs DC tip

Some CPAP machines accept a direct 12V/24V DC cable instead, which skips that conversion step and is typically more efficient. Check your machine's documentation for DC compatibility first.

### LI Post 4 — Refrigerator, "surge vs. continuous" as a design lesson
An underrated engineering distinction that matters a lot in practice: continuous output and surge (startup) rating are two different numbers, and a device can fail on either independently. A refrigerator compressor can briefly draw 2-3× its running watts just to start — so a power station with plenty of continuous capacity can still fail to start a compressor if its surge rating is too low. Our free calculator checks both requirements separately instead of collapsing them into one number.
