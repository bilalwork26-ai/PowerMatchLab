## Script 1 — "Watts vs. Watt-Hours" (the mix-up that gets people the wrong power station)

**Length:** ~35 seconds

**Hook (0–3s):**
Voice: "If you're about to buy a power station, you need to know this ONE thing first."
On-screen text: "1 mistake = wrong power station"

**Scene 1 (3–12s):**
Visual: Split screen — a speedometer icon animates on the left, a battery/fuel gauge fills up on the right.
Voice: "Watts is a RATE — like speed. It tells you if a station can even START your device."
On-screen text: "WATTS = right now"

**Scene 2 (12–20s):**
Visual: The fuel gauge side continues, filling over a clock ticking.
Voice: "Watt-hours is a TOTAL — like distance traveled. It tells you how LONG the station lasts."
On-screen text: "WATT-HOURS = over time"

**Scene 3 (20–28s):**
Visual: Two power stations side by side, one labeled "high W, low Wh," one "low W, high Wh" — both show an X or checkmark against a sample device.
Voice: "Mix these up, and you either can't start your device — or you run out way too fast."
On-screen text: "Check BOTH before you buy"

**CTA (28–35s):**
Visual: Site logo + URL card.
Voice: "Full breakdown and a free calculator — link in bio."

Visual: A 200W panel icon, with an arrow shrinking it down to "~130W" with a percentage badge "65%".

On-screen text: "Realistic output ≈ 60–80% of rated"
